## What each run folder holds

Every run's figures are drawn by the same code from its per-seed records; the best seed is shown in the snapshots (one figure, t = 100 to 10000), the growth figure draws the half-maximum length L_half with its mean +-1 sd, the kinetics figure has panels (a) to (f) and `free_energy_<case>.png` the free energy per site. The `figure_notes.txt` of a folder has, for each of its figures, what it shows and the numbers that are not on the image. `manifest.json` has every number the figures are drawn from; `report.txt` has the model, the composition and its conservation, the solve time, the summary table, the step schedule and the diagnostics.

FNO's own extras (per-case summaries and CSVs, the best seed's folder, the film, diagnostics.json, sweep_curves.npz, the bookkeeping manifest) are not here: they are in `Work/rollout_cache/<run>/extras/`.

## Finite-size regime

Past L > l / 8 (mean zero-crossing length) the domains feel the box; the figures shade that regime and the report's exponents are fitted over the late third of the rollout, so read late numbers at l = 128 with that in mind.

## Regenerate

`python make_submission.py` redraws every complete run from its records and rebuilds this page; `--run LABEL` one run; `--overview` only this page and the comparison figures. No model is run and no trajectory solved.
